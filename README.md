日本的一项小规模临床试验显示，利用“溶瘤病毒”治疗晚期胰腺癌取得了令人鼓舞的结果。在这项研究中，三名患者在直接注射了名为 OBP-301（Telomelysin）的改造病毒后，肿瘤停止了生长。

该疗法采用了一种经过工程化改造的腺病毒，这种病毒通常引起普通感冒。它经过专门设计，旨在靶向并在产生端粒酶的细胞内部进行复制——端粒酶在大多数癌细胞中高度活跃，但在健康的成人细胞中极少见。随着病毒的增殖，它会导致癌细胞破裂，并释放出肿瘤标志物，从而提醒免疫系统识别并攻击剩余的恶性组织。

在这项 I 期试验中，病毒通过内镜被直接注入肿瘤。其中一名患者的肿瘤缩小了 30%，另外两名患者的病情趋于稳定。考虑到胰腺癌极难治疗，这一结果具有重大意义；胰腺癌往往在晚期才被确诊，且受到致密物理屏障（基质）的保护，阻碍了传统化疗的渗透。

尽管早期结果令人振奋，但研究人员强调，该研究的主要目的是测试安全性，且参与人数较少。要确定该疗法的长期有效性以及是否能显著延长生存期，仍需进行更大规模的临床试验。尽管如此，能够遏制晚期病例的病情进展，标志着针对这种最致命癌症之一的治疗取得了潜在突破。

## 4. Claude 解决了黑洞物理学中的两个开放性问题。

**原文标题**: Claude solves two open problems in the physics of black holes

**原文链接**: [https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-black-hole-physics](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-black-hole-physics)

据报道，Claude 解决了理论物理学中关于黑洞和量子引力理论探索的两个长期悬而未决的问题。这些研究结果利用全息对偶框架，架起了广义相对论与量子力学之间的桥梁。

**1. 霍金-佩奇相变**
1983年，史蒂芬·霍金和唐·佩奇证明，加热模型宇宙会导致一种突然的“相变”，使气体突然坍缩成黑洞。虽然这在广义相对论中成立，但物理学家在过去 20 多年里一直难以证明这种突发性是否存在于时空“模糊”的量子领域（弱耦合）。Claude 完成了一项涉及数千个复杂项的大规模“三圈”计算，证明了即使在这种量子状态下，该相变依然是突发的。这表明黑洞的形成是量子理论本身的一个基本特征。

**2. 模自举上限**
在简化的二维模型宇宙中，物理学家使用一种称为“自举”（bootstrapping）的方法来排除不自洽的引力理论。这涉及为该宇宙中最轻黑洞的可能质量设定一个数学上限。通过识别数值数据中的模式并应用球体堆积的数学技术，Claude 为该技术的最简版本计算出了精确且不可逾越的上限。这有助于物理学家缩小寻找自洽量子引力理论的范围。

**研究方法与验证**
Claude 通过由多个 AI 智能体组成的协调小组相互交叉检查数学证明，从而实现了这些结果。研究发现已通过 Lean 计算机证明检查器进行了形式化验证，并随后由人类物理学家进行了评审。虽然这些发现涉及的是理论模型宇宙，但它们为最终统一宏观与微观定律提供了关键见解。

Originally, the myQ app provided functional notifications, but it eventually became "encrusted with ads." The breaking point occurred when the manufacturer, Chamberlain, cut off third-party API access and began sending spam notifications through the app.

To reclaim control, the author replaced the official system with **ratgdo32** devices—$62 WiFi control boards that run open-source firmware and communicate locally via LiftMaster’s Security+ 2.0 protocol. This hardware allows for direct integration with Apple HomeKit, enabling voice commands via Siri and removing any reliance on the manufacturer's cloud.

The author leveraged the device's JSON status endpoint to build a custom monitoring solution. Inspired by a childhood memory of a mercury switch, they wrote a script that sends Slack notifications if the garage door is left open at night. Additionally, the author used Tailscale to advertise specific routes to the devices, allowing for remote health checks and monitoring without compromising security.

The author concludes that the new setup is superior to the original, as it runs entirely locally, is free of advertisements, and provides more detailed notifications (such as identifying who opened the door). The post ends with a harsh condemnation of Chamberlain’s business practices and the decline of the myQ service.

## 8. 98%的老虎机：付费广告如何沦为一种榨取税

**原文标题**: The 98% Slot Machine: How Paid Advertising Became an Extraction Tax

**原文链接**: [https://globalcommercegraph.com/writing/the-ninety-eight-percent-slot-machine](https://globalcommercegraph.com/writing/the-ninety-eight-percent-slot-machine)

In "The 98% Slot Machine," the Global Commerce Graph argues that digital advertising has devolved from a growth engine into a predatory "extraction tax" on businesses. The title refers to the staggering inefficiency of modern ad spend, where the vast majority of capital no longer generates incremental growth but is instead used to defend existing market share.

The article highlights a fundamental shift in how platforms like Google, Meta, and Amazon operate. Originally, these platforms facilitated **discovery**, helping brands find new customers. Today, they focus on **extraction**. A significant portion of ad budgets is now spent on "brand search"—where companies must bid on their own names to prevent competitors from stealing the top search result. This creates a "protection racket" dynamic where brands pay to "acquire" customers who were already looking for them.

Key takeaways include:

*   **The Rent-Seeking Model:** Platforms have commoditized search and social intent, forcing brands to pay for access to their own organic audience.
*   **Cannibalization of Profits:** Much of the data-driven "success" reported by ad platforms is illusory, as ads often target users who would have purchased via organic channels anyway.
*   **The CAC Trap:** As brands bid against one another for the same limited digital "shelf space," Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC) have skyrocketed, often outstripping the lifetime value of the customer.

The article concludes that the current trajectory is unsustainable. To survive the "98% slot machine," businesses must shift their strategy away from over-reliance on paid performance marketing. Instead, they should reinvest in **owned media**, product innovation, and direct customer relationships to escape the cycle of digital rent-seeking and build genuine, incremental value.
