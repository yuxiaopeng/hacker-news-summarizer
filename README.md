## 1. Lovable应用泄露护照信息及20多万条私信

**原文标题**: Lovable apps expose passport details and more than 200K private messages

**原文链接**: [https://www.crissyfield.de/research/lovable-en/](https://www.crissyfield.de/research/lovable-en/)

一份研究报告显示，许多使用 AI 开发平台 Lovable 构建的 Web 应用程序泄露了敏感的个人和企业数据。虽然 Lovable 允许用户通过自然语言提示词创建复杂的应用，但该平台的“无代码”营销宣传与保障数据库安全所需的技术专业知识之间存在严重的安全性差距。

该调查分析了 2025 年至 2026 年间超过 15.9 万个 Lovable 项目。通过对集成 Supabase 数据库服务的项目进行重点研究，调查发现，在 24,664 个站点样本中，约 30% 的站点通过未受保护的 API 请求泄露了电子邮件地址。其他更令人震惊的发现包括：

*   **私人通信：** 超过 20 万条聊天消息和内部 WhatsApp 日志。
*   **身份盗用风险：** 1,000 多条包含护照号码、有效期和国籍的记录。
*   **个人数据：** 一个德国论坛泄露了 8 万个包含全名和出生日期的用户资料。
*   **业务与财务信息：** 数万条涉及门票销售、发票以及公司内部邮件的 AI 生成摘要记录。

核心问题源于访问权限配置错误。尽管 Lovable 宣传其易用性，但其服务条款将数据安全责任完全划归给用户。由于 AI 构建的应用通常遵循重复的结构模式，攻击者能够轻易识别这些漏洞，并同时对多个应用实施攻击。

报告总结道，AI 开发工具无法替代专业的安全审查。报告警告称，在应用程序处理真实数据之前，必须由专家对访问权限和接口进行审计，以防止身份盗用、网络钓鱼和欺诈行为。

## 2. 软件职位谱系图

**原文标题**: A Family Tree of Software Job Titles

**原文链接**: [https://zwischenzugs.com/2026/11/03/a-family-tree-of-software-job-titles/](https://zwischenzugs.com/2026/11/03/a-family-tree-of-software-job-titles/)

在《软件职位家谱》中，Ian Miell 通过梳理从 20 世纪 40 年代至今的 104 个职位名称，探讨了科技行业的演变。该项目将这些职位划分为七大职能家族（如开发、数据/人工智能和运维），追踪了 80 年间各类角色的涌现、融合或消亡。

Miell 指出了行业中几个反复出现的模式：

*   **编程的地位：** 编程在 20 世纪 40 年代最初被视为地位低下的文书工作，随后演变为核心的创意角色。Miell 指出一个讽刺的现象：人工智能现在可能正将编程推回其文书工作的起源。
*   **专业化周期：** 职位头衔经常经历拆分与重组。例如，20 世纪 90 年代的“网站管理员”（Webmaster）拆分成了专门的前端和后端角色，而这些职能后来又在“全栈”头衔下重新合并。
*   **过渡性职位：** 某些角色是短暂的；“提示词工程师”（Prompt Engineer）在技能被其他角色吸收后迅速兴衰，这与“敏捷专家”（Scrum Master）自主权下降的情况类似。
*   **历史重演：** 许多“新”角色只是旧概念的重新包装。现代的“前向部署工程师”（Forward Deployed Engineer）与 20 世纪 60 年代 IBM 的“系统工程师”非常相似，而 DevOps、SRE 与平台工程之间持续的争论，则反映了数十年来为弥合开发与运维鸿沟而进行的博弈。

最终，Miell 认为软件职位名称是流动且周期性的。他指出，了解这些角色的历史对于把握现状至关重要，因为当今所谓的“创新”头衔往往只是行业长期矛盾的最新演变。

## 5. 希捷新款混合硬盘引发了一些老问题

**原文标题**: Seagate's new hybrid drive raises some old questions

**原文链接**: [https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/11/seagate-resurrects-the-ssd-hdd-hybrid-drive/](https://arstechnica.com/gadgets/2026/11/seagate-resurrects-the-ssd-hdd-hybrid-drive/)

Seagate has launched the **FireCuda Forge**, a new hybrid storage line designed to combat skyrocketing hardware costs driven by the 2026 AI boom. The system combines a high-capacity traditional hard drive (HDD) with a user-supplied NVMe SSD, managing them as a single volume through Seagate’s new **Alkemi** intelligent storage platform.

The FireCuda Forge is available in 8TB ($699) and 20TB ($899) configurations. However, these prices do not include the required SSD; users must provide their own M.2 NVMe drive (up to 1TB) with minimum read speeds of 2,400MB/s. The combined system utilizes USB4 and promises speeds of up to 2,500MB/s.

The move marks a return to a storage strategy popular in the early 2010s, most notably seen in Apple’s "Fusion Drive." While hybrid setups offer a cost-effective balance between SSD speed and HDD capacity, they historically carry significant risks. In older systems, the failure of either the SSD or the HDD often resulted in total data loss for the entire volume.

The article notes that Seagate has been vague regarding the technical specifics of the Alkemi platform. While the company markets "intelligent data tiering" that optimizes file placement based on usage, it has not clarified whether the system uses data mirroring for safety or if it is susceptible to the same fragility as past hybrid solutions. Until Seagate releases documentation on failure recovery and data management, the FireCuda Forge remains a high-performance solution shadowed by old questions regarding long-term reliability.

## 7. “灰岩法”：极致无趣带来的生活改善力 (2021)

**原文标题**: 'Grey Rocking': The Life-Improving Power of Being Really, Really Boring (2021)

**原文链接**: [https://www.esquire.com/uk/life/a38473153/grey-rock-technique-being-boring/](https://www.esquire.com/uk/life/a38473153/grey-rock-technique-being-boring/)

“灰岩法”（grey rock technique）是一种旨在应对自恋者、精神病态者和“情绪吸血鬼”等有毒人格者的心理策略。该方法由博主斯凯拉（Skylar）于2012年提出，其核心是刻意表现得平庸无趣且不予回应。通过让自己像自然景观中一颗毫不起眼的“灰色岩石”一样，实践者剥夺了对手渴望看到的戏剧性冲突和情感反应，最终使其失去兴趣并转向新目标。

文章指出，虽然该技术最初是为了应对高风险情境（如逃离虐待关系或应对跟踪者），但对于处理日常烦事同样有效，比如充满火药味的家庭群聊或难相处的同事。使用者不再进行“膝跳反应式”的还击或逗趣，而是给予“也许”或“不知道”等不置可否、且“令人绝望地乏味”的回答。

然而，作者警告了两个主要陷阱：
1. **“核选项”：** 若对真正喜爱的家人或朋友使用，这种方法会显得过于冷酷，从而损害真挚的关系。
2. **自我的迷失：** 长期保持演戏所需的“苦行僧般的克制”，可能导致个人性格和观点的丧失。

最后，文章总结道，只要运用得当，灰岩法就是一种改善生活的“超能力”。它提供了一种简化生活、化解陈年积怨的方法，前提是使用者必须清楚何时该施展此术，何时该回归真实的自我。

## 9. 领英与Monster在CBC调查后撤下逾1000条虚假招聘信息。

**原文标题**: LinkedIn, Monster take down over 1000 fake job postings after CBC investigation

**原文链接**: [https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/linkedin-monster-take-down-over-1-000-fake-job-postings-after-cbc-investigation-9.7415379](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/linkedin-monster-take-down-over-1-000-fake-job-postings-after-cbc-investigation-9.7415379)

A CBC News investigation has led to the removal of more than 1,500 misleading job postings from LinkedIn and Monster. These listings, which promised high-paying remote travel positions with salaries up to $75,000 USD and no required experience, were revealed to be recruitment funnels for Evolution Travel, a U.S.-based multi-level marketing (MLM) company.

The investigation found that unsuspecting applicants were directed to virtual information sessions where they were pressured to pay upfront fees and monthly subscriptions. Rather than receiving a salary, "hired" individuals were expected to earn money through travel commissions and by recruiting more members into the scheme. To attract applicants, agents used various front-company names like Wanderwell Collective and Professional Travel Hub, making no mention of Evolution Travel or the required fees in the initial ads.

While Evolution Travel’s leadership claimed they instruct agents not to post on job boards, CBC found training videos where members specifically taught others how to use LinkedIn to recruit. Experts interviewed by CBC, including former FTC chairman William Kovacic, characterized these practices as deceptive and "disgusting," noting they likely violate consumer protection laws.

In response to the report, LinkedIn removed nearly 900 ads and Monster took down 600, stating that misleading posts and MLM opportunities are strictly prohibited. However, critics point out that the lack of initial vetting on these platforms allowed the scheme to persist for years, eroding job-seeker confidence and turning professional networking sites into "funhouse mirrors" of fraudulent opportunities.
